The objective

Make the region of interest
easier to review.

The project connects ECG segmentation with clinical review. First, the recording is segmented into P, Q, R, S, and T components. Clinical rules then use that structure to localize candidate anomalous segments and generate preliminary annotations, or pseudo-labels.

ECG Verification brings those candidates into a visual workspace where doctors can inspect the waveform, refine the marked region, and add anomaly types. The goal is to support precise annotation, with clinical judgment guiding the final interpretation.

The project workflow

Segment. Localize. Refine.

01

Segment the ECG

Identify the PQRST components to establish the waveform structure used by the next stage of analysis.

ECG recording → PQRST segments
02

Localize candidate anomalies

Apply clinical rules to the segmented signal to propose regions of interest. These pseudo-labels give clinicians a starting point for review.

Clinical rules → candidate regions
03

Refine with clinical judgment

Inspect each candidate in context. Move or resize its bounding box, add missed regions, remove unsuitable annotations, and assign or add anomaly types.

Clinician review → refined annotations
